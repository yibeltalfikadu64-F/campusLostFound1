# User Manual: Injibara Campus Lost & Found

## Welcome to Injibara Campus Lost & Found
This application helps Injibara University students and staff report lost personal items, submit found items, and claim recovered property securely.

---

## 1. Getting Started
1. Open the application.
2. Select your preferred language (English or አማርኛ) at the top right of the Welcome screen.
3. Tap **Log In** if you have an existing account, or **Create Account** to register with your full name, student ID, and phone number.

---

## 2. Reporting a Lost or Found Item
1. Tap the **Report** button in the bottom navigation tab.
2. Choose **"I Lost Something"** or **"I Found Something"**.
3. Fill in the item details:
   - **Title:** e.g. "Samsung Galaxy A54 Blue".
   - **Category:** Select the appropriate category (Electronics, IDs, Wallets, etc.).
   - **Campus Location:** Select where the incident occurred (e.g. Central Library, Cafeteria, Sports Field).
   - **Description:** Provide helpful physical characteristics.
4. **For Found Items (Crucial):** Fill in the **Secret Verification Detail**. This is a private detail only the true owner would know (e.g., lockscreen image, hidden cash amount, scratches, serial numbers). Campus security uses this to verify claims.
5. Tap **Add Photo** to take a picture or attach up to 3 gallery photos.
6. Tap **Publish Item Report**.

---

## 3. Finding & Claiming Items
1. Browse recent campus reports on the **Home** tab or use the **Search** tab to filter by keywords, category, and campus location.
2. Tap on any item card to see full specifications and photos.
3. If you recognize an item as yours, tap **Claim This Item**.
4. Detail your evidence: describe how and when you lost it, answer any secret features, and attach an optional receipt/proof photo.
5. Tap **Submit Claim for Verification**.

---

## 4. Security Review & Collection
1. Campus security officers review submitted claims and check proof against secret details.
2. You will receive an in-app notification under the **Alerts** tab when your claim is approved.
3. Check **My Activity → My Claims** to view the collection appointment location (e.g. Security Office #102) and scheduled time.
4. Bring your physical Student ID to collect your item. Security will confirm the return on the system!
